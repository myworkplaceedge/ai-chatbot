# User Guide - AI Lesson Coach

## Getting Started

The AI Lesson Coach helps you practice communication skills from your Workplace Edge lessons. Every response follows a simple coaching rhythm so you get more than just an answer:

1. It listens and acknowledges what you're dealing with.
2. It teaches the relevant communication skill straight from your lesson material.
3. It offers a concrete way to practice or apply the skill this week.

## How to Access

Navigate to the chat page at your organization's URL (e.g., `https://your-app.vercel.app/chat`).

## Choosing a Topic

When you first open the chat, you'll see a **welcome screen** with topic cards:

- **Give Feedback** -- Learn to deliver constructive feedback
- **Set a Boundary** -- Practice setting healthy limits
- **Push Back on Deadline** -- Negotiate timelines confidently
- **Clarify Tasks** -- Ask the right questions
- **General Coaching** -- Broad communication coaching

Click a topic card to set your focus, then type your question. You can also change topics mid-conversation using the intent bubbles above the input.

## Asking Questions

Type your question in the input box and press **Enter** to send (or click the send button). Good questions include:

- "How do I give feedback to my manager about missing deadlines?"
- "What's the best way to say no to extra work?"
- "How should I ask my coworker to clarify their expectations?"
- "I need help pushing back on an unrealistic timeline"

The AI will only answer from the lesson content uploaded by your instructor. If your question is off-topic, it will redirect you back to the lesson material.

## Follow-Up Prompts

After each response, the AI suggests a follow-up question as a clickable button below the message. Click it to continue the conversation deeper into that skill.

## Practice Mode

Click the **Practice This** button to activate role-play mode. The AI will act as a difficult coworker for 2 exchanges so you can practice your communication skills. After the role-play, it automatically provides coaching feedback on how you did.

## Rating Responses

Each AI response has thumbs-up and thumbs-down buttons. Use these to rate whether the response was helpful. Your feedback helps improve the coaching quality.

## Starting a New Conversation

Click the **New Conversation** button in the top-right corner to reset your session and start fresh.

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Be specific** -- "How do I tell my coworker they interrupted me in the meeting?" is better than "communication help"
2. **Use the topic selector** -- It helps the AI find the most relevant lesson content
3. **Try the follow-up prompts** -- They're designed to help you go deeper
4. **Practice with role-play** -- The best way to learn is by doing
5. **One topic at a time** -- Focus on a single skill per conversation for best coaching

## Important Note

The AI Lesson Coach is a learning tool, not a substitute for HR. For workplace concerns involving harassment, discrimination, or other serious issues, please contact your manager or HR department directly.
