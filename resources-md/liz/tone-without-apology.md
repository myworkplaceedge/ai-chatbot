### Activity 1 -Tone- Liz.pdf

Activity 1
     Reflect on your own language use,
     and answer the following
     questions in the spaces provided.

TAKE A MOMENT TO CONSIDER YOUR INTENTION WHEN YOU USE PHRASES LIKE THIS.
                     ARE YOU TRYING TO BE POLITE?
             SOFTEN AN OPINION THAT MIGHT BE UNPOPULAR?
                           AVOID CONFLICT?

             NOW, CONSIDER THE IMPACT OF YOUR WORDS. AND
    HOW YOU THINK OTHERS PERCEIVE YOU WHEN YOU USE PHRASES LIKE THIS?

### Activity 2- tone-liz.pdf

ACTIVITY 2
    Before we continue please take a moment to reflect on your own
    language

IN YOUR CULTURE, HOW DO YOU USE TONE TO CONVEY RESPECT? WHAT
ABOUT CONFIDENCE? WHAT ABOUT APOLOGY?

CONSIDER THE CULTURE OF YOUR TEAM OR COMPANY. HOW DOES IT
COMPARE TO YOUR HOME CULTURE WHEN IT COMES TO TONE?

 CAN YOU THINK OF AN EXAMPLE WHEN YOU NOTICED A COLLEAGUE’S
 TONE DIDN’T MATCH YOUR EXPECTATIONS FOR WHAT IS APPROPRIATE
 FOR THE SITUATION?
