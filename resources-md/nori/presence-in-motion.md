## Glossary & Vocabulary

### Me_Lesson_32_Nori_Presence_in_Motion_Glossary_and_True-False_Quiz_2025_07_29.docx

**Lesson 32 – Presence in Motion: Glossary Items from Shift Activity and True/False Quizzes**

**1. Eureka!**

**Explanation:** (Greek for "I have found it!") An exclamation of relief or triumph. It is famously attributed to Archimedes. He supposedly shouted it after realizing, while bathing, that displaced water could measure an object's volume. **Example:** "We’re finally able to fix the problem in the water pump! We just need a new gasket. Eureka!"

**True or False:** Eureka is used when an insight or realization is reached after a long, difficult struggle. **(True)**

**2. Lone Wolf Syndrome**

**Explanation:** Lone wolf syndrome describes individuals who often act or work alone, may be socially isolated, and could have anti-social views. **Example:** "We always ask Roger to contribute to our brainstorming sessions, but he’s never even shown up. Lone wolf – unfortunate because we need his input."

**True or False:** Lone wolves are individuals who prefer independence and self-sufficiency rather than rely on others for input or help. **(True)**

**3. Flustered**

**Explanation:** agitated or confused. **Example:** "Before I give an important presentation I breathe deeply and recognize my emotions so that I don’t get flustered.”

**True or False:** Feeling flustered is a good way to stay calm. **(False)**

**4. Mumble**

**Explanation:** say something unclearly and quietly, making it difficult for others to hear. **Example:** "Before an important presentation I remind myself to speak clearly and not mumble so that people can understand my words.”

**True or False:** Mumbling can be a useful tool when speaking in public. **(False)**
